## Project Overview

NutriBasket is a Indian healthy-snacks brand with a strong offline presence that is expanding its focus on online sales.

However, its online marketing efforts are not delivering the results the business expects.

The Marketing Head needs a centralized view of marketing expenditure, sales performance, campaign effectiveness, and channel-level trends to understand what is working and where further investigation is required.

**The objective:** Build an interactive, multi-page Excel dashboard that brings relevant business metrics together and supports data-driven marketing decisions.

## Business Problem

> How can NutriBasket's Marketing Head evaluate marketing performance, identify underperforming areas, and make better-informed decisions about future marketing investments?

The project focuses on exploring questions such as:

- How do sales and marketing performance vary across channels?
- Which campaigns and marketing channels deserve closer investigation?
- How does marketing expenditure compare with business outcomes?
- Which products and categories contribute to sales?
- How does performance change over time?
- What additional analysis could help the business improve its marketing strategy?

The dashboard provides a starting point for these investigations rather than claiming to answer every possible marketing question.

---



## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Loading and Preparation
Imported the source data and prepared it for analysis using Power Query.

### 2. Data Cleaning and Transformation
Applied data preparation techniques to improve consistency and usability.

### 3. Data Modeling
Connected related tables using Power Pivot to create a structured analytical model.

### 4. DAX Measures
Implemented 10+ DAX measures to support business calculations and interactive analysis.

### 5. Dashboard Development
Designed a clean, multi-page Excel dashboard to present selected marketing KPIs, sales trends, and channel performance.

### 6. Business Interpretation
Connected the analysis to the original business problem and identified opportunities for further investigation.
